How it works

  1. Create a room and get an invitation link
  2. Open a video from your device (nothing is uploaded)
  3. Share the link with friends
  4. Watch together with synchronized playback

Your browser plays the file and streams it straight to everyone in the room. MP4 and WebM play instantly; other formats are remuxed or converted in your browser.
